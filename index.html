About me

Background and interests.

I'm a translator and cultural intelligence consultant based in Shanghai and London. I specialize in making complex ideas accessible across languages and cultures. My work spans literary translation, content localization, and strategic cultural consulting.

My approach combines deep linguistic expertise with genuine curiosity about how language shapes thought and culture. I believe translation isn't just about words—it's about preserving meaning, nuance, and intent across linguistic and cultural boundaries.
